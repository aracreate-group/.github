## Who we are

araCreate — founded in 2003 by [Aravinth Panch](https://github.com/AravinthPanch) — has grown from a solo digital studio into a group of companies across Europe and Asia, operating as an interdisciplinary ecosystem delivering 360° services in **Engineering**, **Manufacturing**, **Media**, **Business** and **Training**.

We have supported 300+ clients — from small and medium businesses and social enterprises to unicorn startups, global corporations and government organisations.

## What lives here

This organisation hosts the source code, hardware projects, tooling and internal references maintained by araCreate Group and its teams.

| Area | What you'll find |

## Start here

1. Read [`aracreate-conventions`](https://github.com/aracreate-group/aracreate-conventions) — naming, branching, commit style, licensing and repository structure.
2. Read [`aracreate-guidelines`](https://github.com/aracreate-group/aracreate-guidelines) — how we work together.
3. Use the org [template repositories](https://github.com/orgs/aracreate-group/repositories?q=template%3Atrue) when starting anything new.
